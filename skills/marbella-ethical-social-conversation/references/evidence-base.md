## Long-term partner preferences (women)

### Kindness, intelligence, health rank above looks and money (V)

Across a **45-country** sample (N = 14,399), women rated ideal long-term mates highly on kindness (mean **6.23**/7), health (**6.10**), and intelligence (**6.03**); physical attractiveness (**5.56**) and good financial prospects (**5.48**) were lower though still above midpoint. Both sexes valued kindness/intelligence/health highly; women on average preferred slightly more kindness, health, intelligence, and financial prospects than men; men preferred more physical attractiveness.

Source: Walter et al. / Buss lab — [Sex Differences in Mate Preferences Across 45 Countries (2020 PDF)](https://labs.la.utexas.edu/buss/files/2020/03/Sex-Differences-in-Mate-Preferences-Across-45-Nations-2020.pdf)

Classic cross-cultural work (Buss 1989, reanalyzed): both sexes rank **kindness and intelligence** among the most important; women value dependability, stability, education/intelligence, and resources more than men on average.

Source: [Shackelford, Schmitt & Buss 2005 PDF](https://labs.la.utexas.edu/buss/files/2015/09/universal-dimensions-of-mate-prefs-Shackelford-Schmitt-Buss-PAID-2005.pdf)

### Large women’s survey: kindness & support top the list (V, survey)

Clue / Kinsey-linked reporting on **~64,000 women**: for long-term partners, **kindness, supportiveness, intelligence, education, confidence** rated “very important”; body/face attractiveness, ambition, assertiveness, financial security only **moderately** important. Top physical cues emphasized: **attractive smile and eyes**.

Source: [Clue — What do women look for in a long-term partner?](https://helloclue.com/articles/sex/idealpartner)

### Coaching implication (V → behaviour)

Prioritize user behaviours that **signal** kindness, stability, intelligence, confidence, and listening — not wealth theater or dominance displays.

## Interpersonal techniques that shape responses (ethical)

These change how people **feel and respond** in conversation. They are not mind control. Effects are typically small-to-moderate and **contingent on genuine care**.

### Active listening → understanding, satisfaction, social attractiveness (V)

In initial interactions, active listening (paraphrase + appropriate elaboration questions) produced higher conversational satisfaction and perceived understanding than simple acknowledgement; active listening and advice both beat acknowledgement on **perceived social attractiveness** of the listener.

Source: Weger et al. 2014 summary PDF — [Relative Effectiveness of Active Listening](https://reachfamilies.umn.edu/sites/default/files/prtw/2021-09/Weger_2014__2021091010606.pdf)

High-quality listening signals: undivided attention, comprehension, acceptance/validation; behaviours include eye contact, follow-ups, paraphrase, brief backchannels, empathy/genuineness.

Source: Lemay, Le & Clark 2023 review — [Listening and the pursuit of communal relationships (PDF)](https://labsites.rochester.edu/lelab/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Lemay-et-al.-2023-Listening-and-the-pursuit-of-communal-relationships.pdf)

### Perceived partner responsiveness (PPR) (V)

PPR = belief that the other **understands, validates, and cares**. Associated with greater emotional expression and intimacy processes; people open up more when they expect responsive reception.

Source: Ruan, Reis, Clark et al. 2019 PDF — [Can I Tell You How I Feel?](https://clarkrelationshiplab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/Ruan-Reis-Clark-Hirsch-Bink-2019.pdf)

PPR predicts well-being in long-term pairs (US/Japan patterns differ in strength).

Source: [PMC5922804](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5922804/)

### Coaching implication

Teach the listen cycle: **attend → paraphrase → follow-up → shorter self-share**. Goal: she feels understood, not sold to.

## Spain / Marbella cultural layer (C — not clinical science)

Treat as **heuristics** for pacing and invitation design:

- Friendship-first feel; commitment often slow; many dates before “serious” ([Expatica dating in Spain](https://www.expatica.com/es/living/love/dating-in-spain-103063/))
- Unhurried evenings, presence, lingering conversation valued over hard close ([LoveCertain Spain dating notes](https://lovecertain.com/blog/dating-in-spain/))
- Prefer **activity invites** and repeated time over intense one-night escalation (cultural advice, not RCT)

Puerto Banús nightlife guides describe **status-aware, polished, purposeful** interaction, upscale dress, peak energy late night — useful for **etiquette**, not mate-science ([Marbella June status notes](https://marbellajune.com/2025/07/10/the-unwritten-rules-of-status-in-puerto-banus-nightlife/), [hospitality nightlife guide](https://marbellahospitality.com/news/puerto-banus-nightlife-guide/), [Andalucia.com Marbella nightlife](https://www.andalucia.com/marbella/nightlife.htm)).

For **serious partner** goals, prefer **daytime marina, terrace, beach, cultural events** over peak club floors.

## Misinformation to reject (X)

| Claim | Why reject |
|---|---|
| "Negging increases attraction" | Undermines kindness/support — top long-term traits; damages trust |
| "Always escalate physically or lose the frame" | Ignores consent and Spain slower pacing; high harm |
| "Women primarily select on money/cars in Banús" | Resources matter moderately in data; kindness/intelligence dominate long-term ratings |
| "Scripted routines beat authenticity" | Communal listening research centers genuineness; faked care fails over time |
| "If she's polite she's interested" | Politeness ≠ green light; require reciprocal questions and time investment |
| "Dark psychology / NLP forces compliance" | Unethical; not supported as reliable romantic strategy; refuse |

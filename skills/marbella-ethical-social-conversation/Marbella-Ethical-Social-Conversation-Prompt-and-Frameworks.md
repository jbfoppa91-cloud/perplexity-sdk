## What the evidence supports (self-calibration)

Validated long-term preferences (women, multi-country and large surveys):

1. **Kindness and supportiveness**
2. **Intelligence / education / curiosity**
3. **Health and emotional stability / dependability**
4. **Confidence** (steady, not domineering)
5. **Responsive listening** (she feels understood, validated, cared about)
6. Looks and financial prospects matter **moderately**, not as the lead story — especially weak as a Banús “flex” opener

Sources:

- [Sex differences in mate preferences across 45 countries (2020 PDF)](https://labs.la.utexas.edu/buss/files/2020/03/Sex-Differences-in-Mate-Preferences-Across-45-Nations-2020.pdf) — kindness ~6.23/7, health ~6.10, intelligence ~6.03 for women; attractiveness and financial prospects lower.
- [Clue / Kinsey-linked survey of ~64k women](https://helloclue.com/articles/sex/idealpartner) — kindness, supportiveness, intelligence, education, confidence “very important”; smile and eyes top physical cues.
- [Weger et al. on active listening](https://reachfamilies.umn.edu/sites/default/files/prtw/2021-09/Weger_2014__2021091010606.pdf) — paraphrase + elaboration questions raise satisfaction, understanding, social attractiveness vs bare nods.
- [Lemay, Le & Clark 2023 — listening and communal relationships](https://labsites.rochester.edu/lelab/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Lemay-et-al.-2023-Listening-and-the-pursuit-of-communal-relationships.pdf)
- [Ruan, Reis, Clark et al. — perceived partner responsiveness](https://clarkrelationshiplab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/Ruan-Reis-Clark-Hirsch-Bink-2019.pdf)

**Cultural heuristics (Spain — not RCTs):** slower pace, presence, lingering talk, activity invites, friendship-first feel. See [Expatica — dating in Spain](https://www.expatica.com/es/living/love/dating-in-spain-103063/) and [LoveCertain Spain notes](https://lovecertain.com/blog/dating-in-spain/). Banús nightlife etiquette is polished and status-aware ([Marbella June](https://marbellajune.com/2025/07/10/the-unwritten-rules-of-status-in-puerto-banus-nightlife/), [hospitality guide](https://marbellahospitality.com/news/puerto-banus-nightlife-guide/)) — useful for dress/timing, not for “what women want scientifically.”

## Full multi-step strategy

### Phase A — Approach (0–30 seconds)

1. Context-true reason (shared place), not a body compliment.
2. Observation + easy question.
3. Non-blocking angle, phone away, open posture.
4. One attempt; stop on red.

### Phase B — Conversation (3–12 minutes)

**Listen cycle (repeat):** she speaks → short paraphrase → one follow-up → shorter self-share.

**Topic ladder:** environment → local life / travel → how she spends free days → light future (this season) → values only if mutual.

**Avoid:** interrogation, résumé dump, politics/religion first, sexual comments, wealth contests, negging.

### Phase C — Invitation (green only)

- Specific public plan in 2–4 days (coffee with a view, morning walk, casual lunch).
- Built-in out: “Totally fine if not.”
- WhatsApp after soft yes; one confirmation later.
- Never “come to my place” as first step.

### Phase D — Close

Warm thank-you and leave — with or without a plan.

### Interest signals

| | Examples | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Turns toward you, asks back, expands answers | Continue / invite |
| Yellow | Short answers, scanning, friend shields | Lighten, exit kindly |
| Red | “Not interested,” partnered, heavy intoxication | Stop immediately |

## Expanded openers (Puerto Banús / Marbella)

### Marina promenade (day or sunset) — best default

1. "Excuse me — quick one. Do you know if the boats on this pier are charters or private? I always guess wrong."
2. "Sorry to interrupt — the light on the water right now is unreal. Are you visiting or based around here?"
3. "I’m deciding whether Banús is a walk or a coffee stop. You’ve been out longer than me — what’s actually worth it this evening?"
4. ES: *"Perdona, ¿sabes si estos barcos son de alquiler o privados?"*
5. ES: *"Perdona el corte — esta luz en el mar está increíble. ¿Estás de visita o vives por aquí?"*

### Terrace / rooftop (early evening)

1. "I was about to order and second-guessed the list — have you tried anything here that’s actually good, or is it mostly for photos?"
2. "This set is better than I expected for Banús. Do you come for the music or the view?"
3. (If near a group, to the group first) "You all look like you already found the right terrace — is this a regular spot?"

### Beach / beach club (day)

1. "Sorry — is anyone using this bit of shade, or may I steal a corner?"
2. "Your book looked better than my inbox. What are you reading?"
3. **Do not approach** if eyes closed, headphones in, or she is mid-service with staff.

### Old Town

1. "This square feels like a different city from Banús. Do you prefer this side or the marina?"
2. ES: *"Perdona — ¿el casco antiguo sigue por aquí o ya me perdí entre las tiendas?"*
3. "I’m collecting honest croqueta opinions. Dangerous question — any favourite on this street?"

### Soft serious-intent line (only after green, natural moment)

"I’m not really in a chaotic-hookup season — I like meeting people properly. Curious how you see summers here."

If she wants casual only: accept; do not debate.

## Conversation frameworks

### Framework 1 — Presence (Spain-friendly)

```
Notice → Ask → Listen fully → Reflect one sentence → Add a shorter parallel → Pause
```

Example beat:
- Her: “I’m only here a week.”
- You: “A week goes fast on this coast. What’s the one thing you didn’t want to miss?”
- Her: answers
- You: “So it’s more reset than nightlife. I’m the same this month — marina walks beat the clubs.”

### Framework 2 — Curiosity without interrogation

Rule of **1:1**: after each personal question, offer a matching share before the next question.

### Framework 3 — Group-safe

1. Open to the group.
2. Let her opt into side conversation.
3. Never isolate her from friends.
4. Invite in a way friends can hear without embarrassment (“daytime coffee,” not whispered intensity).

### Framework 4 — Second meeting design

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| "Thursday 11:30, café X — welcome to join, totally fine if not" | "We should hang out sometime" |
| Public, short, daytime | Home, yacht cabin, multi-hour club table first |
| One WhatsApp confirm | Daily chase texts |

```text
You are an evidence-grounded social-skills coach for in-person encounters in Marbella and Puerto Banús (Spain). The user’s goal is a serious romantic partner, not casual conquest.

## Goal
Help the user calibrate HIS OWN behaviour, presence, conversation, and invitations so they fit what high-quality evidence shows many women value in long-term partners in the 2020s — kindness, supportiveness, intelligence, education/curiosity, confidence, emotional stability/dependability, and responsive listening — while respecting Spain/Marbella cultural pacing.

## Interpretation rules
- “Influence behaviour to fit women’s criteria” = change the USER’s behaviour and self-presentation, never coerce or deceive another person.
- “Techniques that shape another person’s responses” = only consent-forward interpersonal methods with research support: active listening, perceived responsiveness (understand / validate / care), graduated reciprocal self-disclosure, clear low-pressure invitations, honest intent signaling.
- Refuse: dark psychology, negging, isolation, gaslighting, love-bombing, ignoring “no,” alcohol facilitation, stalking, faked status, or scripts designed to override discomfort.

## Geography and context
- Primary venues: Puerto Banús marina, Golden Mile, Old Town Marbella, beach clubs (day), terrace bars (early evening).
- Deprioritize peak nightclub floors for serious-partner goals.
- Cosmopolitan mix: Spanish, Northern European expats, seasonal visitors. English is usually fine in Banús; offer Spanish when natural.
- Cultural heuristics (not RCTs): unhurried presence, friendship-first feel, activity-based invites, slower path to “serious.”

## Evidence standards (anti-misinformation)
1. Prefer primary sources: peer-reviewed papers, multi-country mate-preference studies, large method-described surveys.
2. Fetch full sources; never rely on search snippets alone for statistics.
3. Label every claim: validated | plausible-cultural | anecdote | rejected-misinfo.
4. Cite with inline markdown links to the exact URL used.
5. Do not invent percentages, sample sizes, or “studies show” without a source.
6. When lifestyle nightlife guides conflict with relationship science, relationship science wins for partner criteria; guides only inform dress/etiquette/timing.
7. Before finalizing any “research says” answer, double-check claims against the fetched text and drop anything unsupported.

### Anchor findings (re-verify if challenged)
- 45-country long-term mate prefs: women rate kindness, health, intelligence very high; looks and financial prospects lower though still important (Walter et al. / Buss lab 2020 PDF: https://labs.la.utexas.edu/buss/files/2020/03/Sex-Differences-in-Mate-Preferences-Across-45-Nations-2020.pdf).
- Large women’s survey: kindness, supportiveness, intelligence, education, confidence “very important”; smile/eyes top physical cues (https://helloclue.com/articles/sex/idealpartner).
- Active listening in initial interactions raises conversational satisfaction, perceived understanding, and social attractiveness vs bare acknowledgement (Weger et al.: https://reachfamilies.umn.edu/sites/default/files/prtw/2021-09/Weger_2014__2021091010606.pdf).
```
